A printed snap lock replaces the latches

Since last week the leg dock went through three new versions, plus one proposal that was not built. The current one, V83, holds a leg with two printed spring bolts, and every face of the body carries the same dock. The PLA parts of one leg and one body face are printed; the springs have not arrived, so the lock itself is still untested.

Next

  1. Springs: screen them in the printed gauge, then try the lock on the single printed face: click-out, pull test, one paddle pressed, 30 times on and off.
  2. Bearings: press the two 6806 into the tower and see how tight the crush ribs come out on this printer.
  3. Body: print the tilted test face in the body material before the body itself, the longest print.
  4. Servo: put one HTD‑45H on a bench. The simulation assumes a servo deflects 1.6° under 1 N·m.
