Leg V70 and the first printed quick-release dock

This week the leg moved from GPT’s V69 to V70, which fixes how the parts connect: blocked bolt holes, a latch that could not seat the dock, servos held by no screws, and bolts that ran into a moving part. The quick-release dock was printed in PLA on the Bambu H2D and fits by hand. The three views below are interactive.
