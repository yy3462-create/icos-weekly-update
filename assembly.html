    Assembly notes

    • Tighten the M3 screws hand-tight plus 1/8 turn, no more: PLA crushes.
    • Start with the keeper centred in its slots. If the lever will not close, move the keeper 0.2–0.3 mm toward the latch; if there is no click or the leg rocks, move it the other way.
    • Once it is right, scribe a line on the pedestal along the keeper edge, so any slip shows later.
    • Measure S (latch lower screw centre to keeper upper screw centre). The offset δ = S − 14.7 mm sets the final receiver with round holes.

    Part shapes come from the CAD used for this print (dock_print_test). The latch and keeper are simplified from the Southco catalogue dimensions (hole pitch 9.5 / 5.5 mm, S = 14.7 mm); the lever and strap motion is schematic, not the vendor mechanism.
