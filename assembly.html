    M3 hex nut8
    Zip tie, ≤ 2.5 mm wide2

    Notes

    • M3 screws: hand-tight plus 1/8 turn. PLA crushes.
    • The keeper holes are slots (±1 mm). Lever will not close: move the keeper 0.2–0.3 mm toward the latch. No click: move it away.
    • Set the leg down 10 mm ahead of the receiver, then push it back, so the dovetail enters through the full-width opening.
    • The zip tie runs over the lever and through the U tunnel under the latch. Cut it to release.
